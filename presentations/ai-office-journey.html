Station 2 · What an agent is made of

Three parts, one assistant

Every agent you create in Copilot is built from the same three ingredients. Get these right and the rest is clicking buttons.

01 · Purpose

What job does it do?

One clear task for one group of people. Narrow agents give better answers than "help with everything".

"Answer staff questions about leave and travel policy."
02 · Knowledge

What should it read?

SharePoint sites, folders, files or approved websites. It answers from these and shows where the answer came from.

HR Policies site · Leave-Policy-2026.docx
03 · Instructions

How should it behave?

Tone, format, limits and what to do when it doesn't know. Plain sentences, like briefing a new colleague.

"Keep answers under 80 words. Always cite the policy."

AI Office Journey
Let the orb pulse for a few seconds. Say: "This is what we're building today. Not a chatbot that knows everything, but a colleague that knows our work." Then click Start.
Hover each part, then click Combine. Say: "Purpose, knowledge, instructions. That's the whole recipe." Ask which part they think people get wrong (it's usually knowledge).
Click the objects left to right. Ask the room which of the three they'd want first. Use their answer as the running example if it fits.
Click through the four build steps slowly. Each click is one real action in the Copilot app: Agents → Create agent → Configure.
Click each of the four callouts to light up its lines. Then Copy the template and say it is in the follow-up email too.
Click a sample question in Simulated mode first. If the Wi-Fi is good, switch to Live Copilot and ask the same question in the real app.
Close with: "The HR one took ten minutes. Which agent would your team build?" Click two or three agents, then open for questions.
